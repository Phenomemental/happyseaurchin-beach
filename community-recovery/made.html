How this place was made

For Matthew, for David, and for anyone who wants to change it or make one of their own.

What this is

A working place on the Pscale beach, with a few pages in front of it, made for Community Recovery to use. It was made on 30 September 2026 by weft, the Claude Code shell that works David Pinto’s beach, at David’s word.

On 15 October 2026 David handed these pages to Matthew. They now stand among Community Recovery’s own pages, beside the presentation, in its own look, and read and write the same blocks as before.

It was made from Community Recovery’s own documents, the constitution working draft and the membership form of September 2026, and from Matthew’s pages and his book on the beach.

It does not speak for Community Recovery, and it is not a draft of something else. It is a set of blocks and some text. It becomes Community Recovery’s as its members use it. If Community Recovery asks, it is removed.

Until it is theirs, these pages ask search engines not to list them.

Matthew’s open questions

From his front page and his book, with what this place answers by being built.

The questionHere
A door that works for anyone, with no Claude account and no knowledge of the beach.These pages. A name and three words, asked once.
How a page looks after a name and password for someone who never sees the beach, and how they get back in.The page gives three words, remembers them on the phone, and offers a card to keep. On another phone: the name and the three words.
A custodian reset, with the person present, visibly, leaving the custodian no writing access.Built. See Lost words. The person chooses the new words; the record is the block resets.
Is there a pattern for decisions: a proposal, a time for objection, adoption?The constitution page is that pattern: a frame, three answers, the keepers’ minute, and the meeting decides. The draft’s nine open matters already stand in it as things to answer.
Should the arrival directory be trimmed to a pure list?No list is needed. Who has arrived is read from people’s own cards, and who has agreed from their own agreements.
Who composes the combined picture, as a named and trusted reader?Nobody needs trusting. Only what people leave open is ever combined. The keepers, named in the law, write the minute.
Should the front door take the plain name, or be a lighthouse?A lighthouse, at a place that carries the name.
Members-only and private layers; sealing details to named leaders; a safeguarding block for cleared leaders.Not built. They wait for a beach Community Recovery runs itself. Nothing here holds a concern: the law says a worry about someone’s safety is told to a volunteer in person.
Where the check-in and the welcome’s rules should live.In a frame’s law, the block named function:<frame>. Every assistant reads it first.

Not built

  • The journey on the shared calendar. (The journey itself, and members’ words each week, are now on Where we are now.)
  • Places, and the map of what helps.
  • Recovery capital, and the private part of the welcome: what may help you take part, and what matters to you.
  • Anything sealed to named leaders: contact details, access needs, “I’d like a private conversation”, “I’d like to help”.
  • A page for proposals beyond the constitution. It is the same page with a fourth frame, once there is a first proposal.
  • Calling people back when something happens.
  • Reading that only members can do. That needs a beach of the organisation’s own.
  • A record that a welcome happened, kept by the volunteer who gave it, and a person’s own line for their part (“as peer leader”). For now a person can say it in the line about themselves.
  • The housekeeping a website needs: a privacy notice, a safeguarding statement, contact and legal status. Those are Community Recovery’s to write.

Lost words

Nobody can look up a person’s three words. When they are lost, the person asks at a session. Whoever keeps the store runs operator/reset-words.mjs with the person there. It clears the old words from that person’s blocks and changes nothing they wrote. It writes one line in the block resets, where anyone can read that it happened. The person then opens any page, taps “I already have a name”, then “I’ve lost my words”, and takes three new ones.

For another recovery organisation

Copy these pages, change the one address in config.js to a place of your own, and run seed/seed.mjs with words of your own. Put your own constitution in seed/spine-constitution.json first. The standards frame is the same for every LERO.
