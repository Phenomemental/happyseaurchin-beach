Where we stand

Working prototype

Not live yet. This picture is drawn from an earlier version of the standards, so it doesn’t yet include readings written on the new Standards page. It will be switched over once that’s ready. For now, treat it as an early sketch rather than the current picture.

CLERO, the national body for lived experience recovery organisations, sets out 33 standards for what a good LERO looks like. They describe where to get to, not how.

This page is the combined picture. People write their own readings on the Standards page, in their own words: whether each standard is already spoken for, partly there, or not yet. Nobody scores anything. Here, each circle shows what the readings add up to, and where people see it differently, that shows too.

Reading the standards…

    What it means

    Readings are written by members in their own mirrors on the beach, one for each person, at the same place as each standard. This page reads them afresh each time it opens.
