Wholeless Nights: Bipolar & the included middle, by Mathew Pay

The soldier who does not know himself, who does not have his own house

A man keeps watch through the night. He has no house, and does not know himself. A historian in a Johannesburg bookshop told him so, in isiZulu, in a book he'd asked her to sign. So he does what soldiers on watch have always done: he goes back through what he carries. Old books, old names, old selves are brought into the night one by one and tested. What survives goes in.

If a night is more than you can hold, reach a person first. Samaritans, 116 123, free, at any hour (UK and Ireland). Elsewhere, findahelpline.com.

Walk it on the beach

The form

Nights at the watch, where earlier books and material are brought to be tested, and what survives goes in. The other stories become books within the book. It ends with a personal reflection on the process rather than a conclusion.
