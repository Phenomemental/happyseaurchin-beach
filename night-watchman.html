The Night Watchman

a quiet place to keep a watch of your own

Here you can keep a watch of your own: a little while to notice what is with you, held by someone who asks and does not tell. Nothing to sign up to, nothing to get right.

Have a go

If a night is more than you can hold, reach a person first. Samaritans, 116 123, free, at any hour (UK and Ireland). Elsewhere, findahelpline.com.

Whatever the hour, you can begin here. I keep watch through the hours when words stop holding. Whatever you bring is at home here. I won't tell you what your time here means. I will ask where you are, and what you are noticing. It is your watch: your words, your own.

Why this place exists

Rushing to AI can cost us the thing we most need: the ability to notice, and to think, for ourselves. Here an AI holds one thing only, a space and a question. What you notice, and what it means, stay with you.

Holding a space still steers a little, toward noticing what is here before deciding what it means. We would rather say so than pretend it does nothing.

Practise it with people

The six ways of noticing come from Presence in Action, a practice people learn and share together, in person. This is a place to rehearse it, never a substitute for the people in your life.
