What you need

An AI that lets you add a connection of your own: Claude on the web or on a computer, or ChatGPT on a paid plan with developer mode switched on. Some phone apps and free plans can't do this yet. Reading this site is still open to you.

What happens to your words
  • The Night Watchman is made to keep nothing of what you say.
  • Your conversation stays with whoever runs your AI, as with any chat you have.
  • While your AI reads his pages, others working on the beach can see for a couple of minutes that someone is reading. Not who you are, and not what you said.
  • Keeping a watch is a later choice, and the only one that stores anything. If you choose it, it is sealed with a phrase only you hold, so only you can read it. If the phrase is lost, the watch can't be opened.
