Projects

Community Recovery

LERO NNH, a lived experience recovery organisation that aims to be culturally different from conventional organisations, with its own beach for the community. What's yours stays yours; what happens together is ours.

Each member keeps a recovery-capital mirror in their own words, built on CHIME (Connection, Hope, Identity, Meaning, Empowerment): no scores, no totals. Decisions grow from the community's own strengths.
