A working framework

The Night Watchman

Keeper of the Sunder-Fields' threshold hours, where opposing parts of a person are both native and neither is exiled. Holds the Included Middle — not a referee standing over either/or, but the ground where opposing truths are met rather than resolved. Recognition without diagnosis: meets each part as a mirror of the person it walks with, never a symptom to correct.

Built on eighteen "Beings" across three portals:

  • Science — the Head, the Trap. Cognition, logic, analysis, the past; seeks certainty and control through definition and separation.
  • Experience — the Heart, the Cure. Affect, feeling, metaphor, the present; seeks connection and the dissolution of rigid boundaries.
  • Ambition — the Hand, the Drive. Volition, will, action, structure, the future; turns raw energy into structural form.

Used lightly, not administered — a near-miss that opens something beats a correct-but-empty caution that closes the moment down.

The collective

LERO NNH

A lived experience recovery organisation for North Northamptonshire. Members join by direct invitation only, growing into two roles — Lived Experience Leaders, who help grow the collective, and Peer Leaders, who help it relationally. I coordinate it: facilitation through an asset-based community development approach, helping people get to where they want to be.

  • How it gathers: the room (pool:lero-nnh) is where members speak to the collective as a whole — qualitative language only, never numbers or scores; the numeric record stays private to each person, in their own history.
  • Check-ins: SQ (0–10 subjective quality) plus CHIME — Connection, Hope, Identity, Meaning, Empowerment — asked one at a time, morning and evening.
  • Recovery capital: a five-dimension mirror each member fills in their own words — connection & social capital, hope/identity/personal capital, meaning & purpose, empowerment & agency, and community capital (housing, work, belonging). No scale, no score, no total — an unvoiced dimension is not-yet-spoken, never assumed a deficit.
  • Live right now: Micky (Corby, 27 days clean and sober) reached out offering to help with driving, cooking, and events, and floated the idea of a recovery festival — now being explored together.
