Of commissioners asked whether their area had a LERO, 41% misreported it: 24 said there was one when there was none, 7 said there was none when there was one.

The answer
RAND’s six recommendations

Each recommendation, answered on one ground

Make clear what a LERO is: each LERO shows itself against the standards, in its own words. Support grassroots LEROs to start: a ready kit of welcome, agreement, constitution, standards and journey. Fund LEROs for the longer term: evidence that builds over years, never a snapshot. Bring LEROs into governance: decisions held and kept in the LERO's own room. Respect between LEROs and treatment: one ground both can read, each keeping its own account. LEROs collect and share their value: days told in members' own words, with no forms to fill.

RAND Europe for NIHR, 2026, chapter 6.

No one watched

Who sees what

Rings for a member, their peer leader, the LERO and CLERO. Whoever is looking, what is sealed from them shows hatched: a member's day and growth go outward only with their yes; names stay on paper; CLERO sees counts only in the fold.

The proposal's own design: each person holds their own account under their own key; what is private is sealed, what is shared is chosen.

How it is paid for

Members pay nothing

Commissioners, public bodies, funders and national programmes fund the service; members pay nothing; a share returns to Community Recovery. The first price is still open, to be set with CLERO.

The economy as the proposal sets it out: members pay nothing, those who need the evidence fund the service, and a share returns to the LERO the kit was shaped with.
