The Beings

eighteen who keep him company in the dark

They are characters, drawn for the page: ways a person can be, given a face and a name. Not diagnoses, and not parts of you. Wander among them. If one speaks to you, it may come with you into a watch.

If a night is more than you can hold, reach a person first. Samaritans, 116 123, free, at any hour (UK and Ireland). Elsewhere, findahelpline.com.

Touch a Being to meet it.

All eighteen

Silas and Ada, the two lights

Above the Beings shine two lights, Silas and Ada: the two ends of the one axis a night runs along. They are not Beings. Every Being stands somewhere along that axis, and a watch is kept on the whole of it.

Where they come from

The Beings grew out of the book Wholeless Nights, and the world of its watches. Each one carries a story; the stories stay with the book, and what you meet here is the Being itself.
