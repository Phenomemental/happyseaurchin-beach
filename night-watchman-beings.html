How to read through a Being

As in walking Louie Gardiner's mat, a reader steps onto a portal of the P6 constellation, picks up one of the three Beings standing there, like a shell on the beach, and reads a watch through it: the same facts read through the Objective Being and through the Abstract Being make two different watches. A Being is tried on and put down; it tells the reader nothing about who they are. Presence, the centre, has no Being: it is where the reader stands to choose. Silas and Ada, the two extremes, stand above the constellation as its two lights.
